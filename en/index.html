A cleaner's first job:
never delete the wrong thing

  1. Whitelist + a second check

    Every deletable path passes a whitelist and a protected-paths list, and is re-validated right before the deletion runs. Irreversible deletions (simulators) require their own confirmation.

  2. Trash first

    Everything except simulator runtimes goes to the Trash — regret has an undo. Until you empty the Trash, not a single file on disk is gone.

  3. Honest numbers

    "Moved to Trash" and "actually freed" are measured separately; freed space only counts what the disk really gave back.

  4. Sandboxed

    The Mac App Store build runs inside the system sandbox and works only inside the Home folder you authorize — macOS enforces the boundary.

FAQ

What does it require?

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. The app has no data-collection network calls; scanning, cleaning and history all happen on your machine. Purchases go through Apple's App Store — we never see your payment details.

What if I delete something by mistake?

Nearly everything goes to the Trash first — drag it back out. Only iOS simulator runtimes (rebuilt on demand) are deleted directly, each with its own explicit confirmation.

Which languages?

English and Simplified Chinese, following your system language.

How do refunds work?

Purchases go through the App Store, so refunds follow Apple's standard process: reportaproblem.apple.com.
