Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

The short version: we don't collect your data

Everything Mac House Clean ("the app") does — scanning, cleaning, duplicate finding, leftover analysis, history — happens locally on your device. The app has no server-side component and never transmits your files, file names, scan results or usage behavior to any server.

What data we handle, and where it lives

Entirely on-device: scan results, cleaning history, trial state and cumulative cleaned totals, license information (including the device code), and preferences. All of it lives in the app's local container and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Keychain/passwords, Mail, Contacts and other sensitive paths are hard-refused: the app never reads or displays them, and they never appear in any scan result.

Permissions

The Mac App Store build runs inside the macOS sandbox. To scan and clean inside your Home folder, the app asks once for Home-folder access; that grant is stored on-device only as a security-scoped bookmark and can be re-authorized or revoked at any time.

Purchases & payments

Purchases are processed by Apple's In-App Purchase system. Payment details are handled by Apple; neither the app nor the developer can access your payment credentials. Order-related information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Third-party components

The app embeds no analytics SDKs, no ad SDKs, no third-party data-collection components, and uses no third-party crash reporting service.

Your rights

Because there is no server-side data, you never need to ask us to "delete your data" — uninstalling the app removes all of it. Questions about this policy: use the contact link in the footer.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update this page and note it inside the app.
